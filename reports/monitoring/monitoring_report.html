Model monitoring: replay of simulated production

42 weekly runs from 08 Jan 2026 to 22 Oct 2026, each using only what was known that day. The simulator injected a drift on 01 Mar 2026: more online and wallet payments, and a new wallet fraud pattern. The monitoring did not know it.

  • Decision rates alerted first, 46 days after the drift, but the same rules had alerted once before it. The label rules, silent before the drift, alerted after 67 days and recommended retraining after 74; recall on mature cohorts only after 123.
  • Input drift with the conventional 0.10 threshold never caught it: the largest weekly PSI of a stable input after the drift was 0.090 (before it, 0.022).
  • A rolling reference (post hoc) would have caught that step after 11 days with no false alarm in 22 drift-free weeks. But that step is the change of legitimate behaviour, not the new fraud.
